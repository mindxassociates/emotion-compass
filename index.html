Step 3

Listen to the body

Emotions often arrive as physical sensations before they become words. These patterns are clues, not diagnoses, and they vary from person to person.

Step 4

Explore the layers

A primary emotion is the first response to an event. A meta-emotion is how you feel about that feeling—for example, ashamed of sadness or afraid of anger. Neither layer is “wrong.”

Your reflections stay in this browser unless you print or save them. Avoid entering identifying or sensitive information on a shared device.

Use this gently

Emotion words and body cues are invitations to notice—not rules. Several emotions can coexist, and the “right” label may change as you slow down. If you are at risk of harming yourself or someone else, or cannot stay safe, seek immediate local emergency or crisis support.
